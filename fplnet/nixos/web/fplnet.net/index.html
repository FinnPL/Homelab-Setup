Peering

Open peering at these exchanges, and on their route servers. IPv6 only.

  • To request a session, mail noc@lippok.eu with your ASN and the exchange. A PeeringDB record is all we need. MD5 is optional.
  • We announce only AS218255's own prefixes, registered as route6 objects in the RIPE database (RIPE::AS218255, no AS-SET). Max prefixes: 10.
  • We drop RPKI-invalid routes, bogon prefixes and ASNs, prefixes longer than /48, and AS paths over 64 hops. We honour graceful shutdown (RFC 8326).
